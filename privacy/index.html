English

In short: Quiete collects no data. There's no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising, and what you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your journal (sessions, calm ratings and notes) and the mixes you save are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Like other app data, they're part of your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, which Apple manages under your account.
  • Settings such as language and timer live in the app's preferences on the iPhone.
  • So the widgets can show your week, a short summary sits in the iPhone's keychain: minutes listened over the last 14 days, average calm, number of sessions, when the current session ends, the language, the last sound you played and whether it's playing, the names of your saved mixes, the widget timer you chose, and whether Quiete Complete is unlocked. It doesn't sync to iCloud Keychain, and only Quiete and its widgets can read it.

What the app doesn't do

  • It has no account and no server, and it makes no network connections. Links to studies open in your browser, which follows its own policy.
  • The one exception is the App Store, if you unlock the full app with the one-time purchase. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy: we never receive your payment details or your Apple Account.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code (SDKs).
  • No access to the microphone, camera, location, contacts or Health data.
  • Nobody else sees your data, so there's nothing to sell or share.

Deleting your data

You can erase the journal in the app's Settings, which also clears the widget summary. Deleting the app removes what it stored; iOS may keep the widget summary in the keychain, where only Quiete can read it.
