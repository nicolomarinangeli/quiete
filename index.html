Quiete for iPhone

Binaural beats, sleep sounds and slow breathing

Quiete is an iPhone app that plays binaural and isochronic beats, 432 Hz and solfeggio tones, rain and brown noise, and guides you through five breathing patterns. Every sound is generated live on the phone, so nothing loops, and nothing you do leaves it.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone with iOS 26 or later, in English, Italian, Spanish, French and German.

What's inside

Six guided sessions

Calm anxiety, Ease agitation, Release tension, Quiet racing thoughts, Drift to sleep and Five-minute reset. Each lays a binaural beat over rain, ocean waves or soft noise. In five of them the beat glides slowly down; Drift to sleep goes from theta to a 3 Hz delta beat over twenty minutes. Release tension walks you through progressive muscle relaxation, one muscle group at a time, and four sessions bring a breathing orb to follow.

Tones, beats and soundscapes

Ten single tones from 174 to 963 Hz (432 and 528 Hz included) and the five brainwave bands from delta to gamma. Underneath, eight soundscapes: rain, ocean, night shore, storm shelter, a warm drone, and white, pink or brown noise.

A mixer you control directly

Pick a binaural beat, isochronic pulses (they work on the speaker too) or a pure tone, then give rain, waves, the drone and each noise its own fader. You hear every change as you make it, and Save as a mix keeps the result for next time.

Five ways to breathe

Cyclic sighing, coherent breathing at six breaths a minute, a longer exhale, 4-7-8 and box breathing. Watch the orb grow and shrink, or close your eyes and let a light tap from the phone mark each change of breath.

Journal and widgets

Sessions longer than a minute land in the journal, where you rate how calm you feel, add a note if you like and see your week. Three Home Screen widgets show the session that fits the hour, your four needs and your week.

What the research says

Sessions and most sounds in Quiete have a card with the level of evidence and links to the 22 published studies behind them. Where the evidence is thin, the app says so. A few of those studies:

  • Binaural beats: a meta-analysis of 22 studies found a medium effect on memory, attention, anxiety and pain (Garcia-Argibay et al., Psychological Research, 2019). Whether the brain actually follows the beat is still open: in a review of 14 EEG studies, 5 said yes and 8 said no (Ingendoh et al., PLOS ONE, 2023).
  • Breathwork: across 12 randomised trials with 785 adults it lowered stress with a small-to-medium effect (Fincham et al., Scientific Reports, 2023).
  • Cyclic sighing: five minutes a day for a month improved mood more than mindfulness meditation (Balban et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2023).
  • Noise at night: in a sleep lab, pink noise left on all night shortened dream sleep, while foam earplugs blocked most outside noise (Basner et al., Sleep, 2026). That's why plain noise in Quiete stops after an hour unless you pick No limit.
  • 432 Hz and solfeggio: only small pilot studies so far (Calamassi and Pomponi, Explore, 2019). The app labels solfeggio tones as tradition, not research.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need headphones?

For binaural beats, yes: each ear hears a slightly different tone and the beat only forms when both reach you. Isochronic pulses, pure tones and the soundscapes work on the iPhone speaker.

Does it keep playing with the screen locked?

Yes. Quiete keeps playing on the lock screen with a timer and a slow fade-out, and it can play over music from another app.

Why does the noise stop after an hour?

A 2026 sleep-lab study found that pink noise left on all night shortened dream (REM) sleep. Plain noise therefore starts with a one-hour timer. You can still choose No limit.

Does Quiete collect my data?

No. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no server. Your journal and your mixes stay on your iPhone. The privacy policy has the details.

What happens to my journal when I change iPhone?

The journal is part of your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, so restoring the new iPhone from that backup brings it back. There's no copy anywhere else.

Is Quiete a medical device?

No. It's made for relaxation and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If anxiety or poor sleep is wearing you down, talk to a doctor.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Italian, Spanish, French and German. It follows your iPhone's language, and you can switch it in the app's settings. This site is also in Italian.
